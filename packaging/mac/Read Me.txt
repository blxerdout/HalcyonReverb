REBLXERD (beta) by CreativeSpxce
================================

INSTALL
1. Double-click "Install Reblxerd.pkg" and follow the steps.
   It installs:
     VST3  ->  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Reblxerd.vst3
     AU    ->  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Reblxerd.component
     App   ->  /Applications/Reblxerd.app (standalone, no DAW needed)
2. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins if it doesn't show up.
   Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan (turn on "Use VST3" / "Use Audio Units").
   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins.

IF MACOS SAYS IT CAN'T BE OPENED
This beta isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS may block the installer the first time.
  - Right-click (or Control-click) "Install Reblxerd.pkg" and choose Open, then Open again.
  - On newer macOS: try to open it once, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security,
    scroll down and click "Open Anyway".

UNINSTALL
Delete the three items listed above.
