REBLXERD (beta) by CreativeSpxce

This installs:
  VST3  ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Reblxerd.vst3
  App   ->  C:\Program Files\Reblxerd\Reblxerd.exe (standalone, optional)

After installing, open your DAW and rescan plug-ins:
  Ableton Live: Options > Preferences > Plug-Ins > turn on "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders", then Rescan
  FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": this beta isn't code-signed yet.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

To uninstall: Settings > Apps > Reblxerd > Uninstall.

Feedback: please include your Windows version, DAW and version, what you did,
and what happened.
